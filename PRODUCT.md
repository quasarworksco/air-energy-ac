# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS. Everything lives in `index.html` (CSS and JS inline), images in `assets/`. Published by GitHub Pages from `main`. Fixed by the project instructions (CLAUDE.md).

## Users
Homeowners and businesses in Houston, Texas and surrounding areas who need air conditioning or heating work, often with urgency (a unit that stopped cooling in Houston heat). Spanish and English speakers; the site is bilingual ES/EN with a toggle. Exact audience split (residential vs. commercial, Hispanic vs. Anglo) is not confirmed.

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for Air Energy AC (client of DGP Global Group, account: Moya Company). Success is a visitor calling the company or sending the quote form.

## Positioning
"Your Trusted Company": a licensed Texas contractor (TACLB84520), BBB A+ rated, accredited since 1993. These come from the company's van wrap and the client confirmed they may be shown.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive mostly on a phone. Primary actions, in order: call, send the quote form. WhatsApp is a secondary channel through a floating button. Service is at the customer's location; there is no storefront address.

## Product Principles
1. The phone number is the product: reaching a person must take one tap from anywhere on the page.
2. Trust comes from verifiable facts (license, BBB accreditation), never from invented proof.
3. Both languages are first class; nothing is left untranslated.
4. Friendly, not corporate-cold: the mascot carries the personality.
